NetZeroCalc privacy notice

NetZeroCalc is an open-source portfolio project.

  • Guest mode: data stays in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • Accounts (email or Google sign-in): we store your email address and the projects you create, in a Supabase database. Each account can read and write only its own rows.
  • Google sign-in requests only your basic profile and email address, used to identify your account. Nothing else from your Google account is read.
  • AI copilot: the question you type and the screen context you send are passed to Groq to generate an answer. Do not enter confidential data.
  • We do not sell data or use it for advertising. To delete your account and data, open an issue at https://github.com/arunachalamvenkatachalapathy-dev/netzerocalc-ai/issues.
